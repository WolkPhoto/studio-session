Contact Sheets integration update

- Removed the separate Workspace Folders selection popup.
- Workspace sidebar folders can be clicked to add their images to Contact Sheets.
- Workspace sidebar folders can be dragged into the Contact Sheets image area.
- Center column displays image thumbnails by default, with optional PDF preview toggle.
- Duplicate images are ignored when adding folders repeatedly.
- External images and PDF export remain available.

Build by double-clicking 00_BUILD_DIGI_SUITE.command.
This is an unverified development build; compile and test on macOS before distributing.
